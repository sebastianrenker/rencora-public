A JARVIS-style, voice-driven holographic AI assistant.

An assistant you can talk to, see and gesture at — running headless in the background with a web dashboard for PC, phone or projector.

View on GitHubRead the README

What it does

Voice

Live voice via Google Gemini with a wake word or continuous listening.

Holographic surfaces

Home hub, table/screen projection, webcam hand tracking.

3D globe and info wall

Satellite imagery, weather, news, ISS and air-traffic layers.

Documents

Reads photos, PDFs and Word files and explains them plainly.

Plugins

Morning briefing, translator, timers, smart home and more.
